🎓 Research Methodology & Academic Citation
Master's Thesis Research Project

Diachronic Urban Mapping & Morphological Analysis of Old Mosul

This web-GIS platform presents research digitizing, reconstructing, and analyzing the urban fabric of Old Mosul across 14 historical eras spanning 637 CE to 2020 CE.

Primary Methodology: Space Syntax (Rₙ), Fractal Dimension (D_f), GIS Vectorization
Coordinate Reference System: WGS84 / EPSG:4326 (GeoJSON RFC7946)
Dataset Layers: 96 Vector Layers Across 14 Chronological Eras
3D Terrain Baseline: High-Resolution Old Mosul DEM
